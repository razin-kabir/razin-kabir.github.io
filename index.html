About

Most of my career has been at the International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI) in Dhaka, where I started as a Research Analyst in 2016 and left in 2026 as Senior Program Manager in the Poverty, Gender, and Inclusion Unit. Along the way I served as Deputy Chief of Party for the USAID-funded Feed the Future Bangladesh Research Activity and as Bangladesh Country Coordinator for CGIAR's Rethinking Food Markets Initiative.

I've worked on every stage of a study: design, protocols and ethics review, survey programming, managing survey firms across multiple rounds, data cleaning, and analysis using randomized controlled trials, difference-in-differences and panel methods. I've also done key informant interviews and focus group discussions with government and private sector stakeholders and with households in remote communities.

My training is in public health, with a Master of International Public Health and a Bachelor of Medical Science from the University of Sydney. I moved to Saskatoon in 2026 and continue to consult for IFPRI remotely.

Fish and shrimp value chains after Covid-19

First-authored journal article, 2023

How the pandemic affected business continuity and resilience among fish and shrimp value chain actors in Bangladesh, based on two rounds of phone surveys with 1,751 value chain actors. Farm clusters turned out to be negatively correlated with resilience under covariate shocks. The study was part of a larger project on Covid-19 and value chains in Bangladesh, Burkina Faso and Uganda.

Published in
Applied Economic Perspectives and Policy 45(4)
Presented

Census of Bangladesh's public foodgrain storage facilities

Led the census, 2017, under the Integrated Food Policy Research Program

A census of every public foodgrain storage facility in Bangladesh, assessing their condition and estimating repair needs. I later built and deployed an R Shiny dashboard that puts the census on a map with a searchable table of facilities. A companion working paper estimated grain losses in the public distribution system using primary survey and secondary data.

Built with
R Shiny, Leaflet
Funded by
World Bank, through a project of the Bangladesh Ministry of Food

Bangladesh Millers' and Traders' Survey

Survey executed in 2018

A nationally representative survey of the rice value chain. The data fed into estimates of private sector rice stocks and a stochastic spatial equilibrium model of how price stabilization policies affect the rice market.
